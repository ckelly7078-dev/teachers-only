## Existing backend handoff

Load `Engine.from_bundle(checkpoint_root)` once in a trusted backend. The active item fingerprint and eligibility mode must be passed to `structured_math_fm17f.check_work(engine, item_id, mode=..., item_fingerprint=..., work=...)`. `specifications(item)` describes the accepted field layout and reference values from the trusted question; it is private solution context, not a learner-facing answer or a parser. The work object contains exactly one field per rubric criterion. Exact rational quantities are strings, arrays encode ordered operands or matrices, and operation identifiers are fixed labels. Null means unavailable or unrecognized evidence and produces uncertainty. Reject invalid shapes or unsupported prompt/rubric revisions. Do not accept an arbitrary mathematical expression for execution.

The algebra checks support recovering signed monic root invariants, shifting, scaling, squaring, ratios, reciprocals and combined transformations. A correctly evidenced method can retain credit after a supplied earlier arithmetic slip; a reciprocal step with zero product cannot. Accuracy remains tied to the original question, not the consequential value. The complex checks distinguish conjugate pairs, unordered complete root sets, expansion, rationalization and real/imaginary components. Matrix checks distinguish a basis-image column construction, first-row cofactor method, singularity classification, absolute area scaling, point-image multiplication and isolation of a zero-matrix equation. Vector checks distinguish cross-product order, nonzero normal verification, distributivity, a specifically requested vector line representation and equivalent plane representations. The latter accepts any base point on the same plane and two independent spanning directions in that plane. Verification of a different reported pair of directions remains uncertain. Explicit restrictions in the prompt and rubric continue to apply.

These are bounded mathematical observations supplied by a parser or reviewer, not interpretation of handwritten or arbitrary free-text work. Unsupported valid methods must be supplied as null, never forced into an incompatible field and called incorrect. No claim of universal mathematical marking is made. Methods and intermediate evidence are checked separately from final accuracy; no learner grade or approval is produced. The earlier official source-route model and distinction between official M/W/MW and original M/A/R remain unchanged.
